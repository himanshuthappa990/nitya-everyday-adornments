Why Nitya exists

Most Indian jewelry is designed for festivals and weddings. We started Nitya because we wanted pieces that feel natural on a Monday morning — light enough to forget, beautiful enough to notice.

Every design is tested for all-day comfort, skin safety, and real Indian weather. We work with small workshops that still hand-finish details you can feel.
